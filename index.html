HOW TO PLAY

The deal

Buy-in. Your stake joins everyone else's at the same table. $5 players only ever meet $5 players.

Everyone carries their stake. Kill someone and you take what they were holding — their buy-in plus everything they had already collected.

House cut. The platform keeps 20% of every payout, in both games. Amounts shown on screen are always what you actually receive.

MAXWIN

Survive to the last. The whole pot goes to the final survivor — or is split evenly between the last team in Duo and Trio.

Loot from a kill drops on the ground as a gold crate: walk over it to collect. Anyone can steal it first.

Duo / Trio: no friendly fire. Everyone respawns 5s after dying, in every mode, until their lives run out.

RESURGENCE

50 brawlers, gas closing twice as fast, 150 boxes. Kills pay straight into your bucket — nothing drops on the ground.

Cash out whenever you dare with the green button (C on keyboard). You leave with your bucket, minus the house cut.

The button locks for 10 seconds whenever you take damage or land a kill. No hit-and-run.

Die and you lose everything — bucket included.

Fighting

Ammo. Three shots, reloading one at a time. Watch the pips above your head.

Super. Charges as you deal damage. Every brawler has a different one — burst, slam, fire pool, heal, stealth, dash, turret.

Smoke grenade. You start with none. They come out of crates, about one in five, and you can carry two at a time. Die and whatever is left drops on the ground for whoever gets there first. Tap G and it drops at your own feet: the cloud closes over you, whoever was shooting loses you, and you walk out. Hold G instead, aim, and release: it flies to where you are pointing, to screen off a lane without hiding yourself. The grenade button next to your super does the same, tap or drag.

The first 4 seconds are yours. A fresh cloud is one-way: your team looks straight through it while nobody can look back in. The badge above the grenade button counts it down. After that the cloud blinds everyone alike for the rest of its 7s, you included, and your screen fogs over until you walk out of it. Anyone else caught inside sees the same white-out from the first second. Standing in their fresh smoke is the same trap in reverse, and the badge turns red to say so.

Bullets always fly straight through smoke. It buys sight, a retreat or a flank, never a free kill. Nothing refills on its own: once you have thrown your last one, the only way to get another is to break more crates.

Power cubes. Break boxes for cubes: each gives +10% max health and +10% damage, up to 10. Killers inherit them.

Speed is earned. A brawler is only fast if it is fragile or fights from range: Brick (200 hp, reach 4.6) walks at 5.65 while Ghost (95 hp) runs at 8.10. Tanks trade mobility for the right to stand still and win a brawl.

Bushes hide you until someone gets close. Walls block shots — except Volt, who lobs bombs over them.

The gas shrinks the map and damages a percentage of your health per second. The screen edge flashes green while it burns you, and the safe circle is drawn on the minimap.

Controls

WASD move · mouse to aim · LMB attack · RMB or Space super · G tap to smoke yourself, hold to aim and throw · Q auto-aim shot · C cash out · Esc pause

Touch: left thumb moves · right thumb taps for a quick auto-aimed shot, or drags to aim and releases to fire · the orange button fires your super (drag it to aim) · tap the grenade button to smoke yourself and get out, or drag it to place the cloud elsewhere.
