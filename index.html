YOUR PROFILE

2 to 14 characters. Shown above your head and in chat.

HOW TO PLAY

The deal

Buy-in. Your stake joins everyone else's at the same table. $5 players only ever meet $5 players.

Everyone carries their stake. Kill someone and you take what they were holding — their buy-in plus everything they had already collected.

House cut. The platform keeps 20% of every payout, in both games. Amounts shown on screen are always what you actually receive.

MAXWIN

Survive to the last. The whole pot goes to the final survivor — or is split evenly between the last team in Duo and Trio.

Loot from a kill drops on the ground as a gold crate: walk over it to collect. Anyone can steal it first.

Duo / Trio: no friendly fire, and you respawn 8s after dying as long as a teammate is still alive.

RESURGENCE

50 brawlers, gas closing twice as fast, 150 boxes. Kills pay straight into your bucket — nothing drops on the ground.

Cash out whenever you dare with the green button (C on keyboard). You leave with your bucket, minus the house cut.

The button locks for 10 seconds whenever you take damage or land a kill. No hit-and-run.

Die and you lose everything — bucket included.

Fighting

Ammo. Three shots, reloading one at a time. Watch the pips above your head.

Super. Charges as you deal damage. Every brawler has a different one — burst, slam, fire pool, heal, stealth, dash, turret.

Power cubes. Break boxes for cubes: each gives +10% max health and +10% damage, up to 10. Killers inherit them.

Speed is earned. A brawler is only fast if it is fragile or fights from range: Brick (200 hp, reach 4.6) walks at 5.65 while Ghost (95 hp) runs at 8.10. Tanks trade mobility for the right to stand still and win a brawl.

Bushes hide you until someone gets close. Walls block shots — except Volt, who lobs bombs over them.

The gas shrinks the map and damages a percentage of your health per second. A green arrow points you back to safety.

Controls

WASD move · mouse to aim · LMB attack · RMB or Space super · Q auto-aim shot · C cash out · Esc pause

Touch: left thumb moves · right thumb taps for a quick auto-aimed shot, or drags to aim and releases to fire · the orange button fires your super (drag it to aim).
